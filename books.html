The LaRosa Sisters series

Catherine and her younger sister, Susanna Mae, grow up a little with each book. Here is the whole series in reading order, across both publishers.

Book 1 · 2020

Baby Jesus Is Missing

Ambassador International · Hardcover, 44 pages

In the small town of Reedy River, baby Jesus figurines are vanishing from Nativity scenes all over town in early December. When Catherine and her younger sister, Susanna Mae, hear about a $500 reward, they set out to catch the thief, and find the case asks more of them than they expected.

The Offering

Ambassador International · Paperback and hardcover

On a September visit to their grandparents’ house in the mountains, the sisters turn up a rusted tin of money. Then their border collie, Atticus, takes off after a bear cub into the woods their grandfather told them to stay out of, and the girls have to decide what to do.
