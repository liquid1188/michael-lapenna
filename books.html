Book 1 · 2020

Baby Jesus Is Missing

Ambassador International

In the small town of Reedy River, baby Jesus figurines are vanishing from Nativity scenes all over town in early December. When Catherine and her younger sister, Susanna Mae, hear about a $500 reward, they set out to catch the thief, and find the case asks more of them than they expected.

  • Hardcover, 44 pages
  • Illustrated by Chris Jackson
  • Reading age 5 to 12

Book 2 · 2023

The Offering

Ambassador International

On a visit to their grandparents’ house in the mountains, the sisters turn up a rusted tin of money. Then their border collie, Atticus, takes off after a bear cub into the woods their grandfather told them to stay out of, and the girls have to decide what to do.

  • Paperback, 62 pages
  • Reading age 4 to 8

Book 3 · 2025

Angelina Grace

Ambassador International

One autumn afternoon Catherine comes upon a young woman living alone in a secluded hollow outside town. As the sisters get to know their new friend, Ulga, they are drawn into the questions surrounding her, and into a decision about her safety.

  • Paperback, 164 pages
AmazonPublisher
Book 4 · 2026

Laura’s Locker

En Route Books and Media

Susanna Mae watches a classmate get framed for cheating and has to find the courage to tell the truth. Along the way, a visit to her grandparents in Gloucester, Massachusetts, brings mysterious woods and old legends.

“Michael LaPenna invites young readers into a compelling encounter with conscience, courage, and grace. Laura’s Locker reveals how faith strengthens the heart to confront injustice, embrace truth, and discover the healing power of authentic friendship.”

Dr. Sebastian Mahfood, OP
