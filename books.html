Book 1 · 2020

Baby Jesus Is Missing

Ambassador International

Early in December, Nativity figures begin disappearing across Reedy River. When a $500 reward is offered for catching the thief, Catherine and Susanna Mae decide to investigate. What begins as a Christmas mystery soon becomes a test of their judgment.

  • Hardcover, 44 pages
  • Illustrated by Chris Jackson
  • Reading age 5 to 12

Book 2 · 2023

The Offering

Ambassador International

While visiting their grandparents in the mountains, Catherine and Susanna Mae discover a rusted tin full of money. Then their border collie, Atticus, chases a bear cub into woods their grandfather has warned them to avoid. Should they obey his warning or go after their dog?

  • Paperback, 62 pages
  • Reading age 4 to 8
AmazonBarnes & NoblePublisher
Book 3 · 2025

Angelina Grace

Ambassador International

One autumn afternoon, Catherine meets Ulga, a young woman living alone in a secluded hollow outside town. As the sisters get to know her, they begin to wonder about her circumstances and what they can do to help her stay safe.

  • Paperback, 164 pages
  • Reading age 8 to 12
AmazonPublisher
Book 4 · 2026

Haddie’s Locket

Independently published

When the girls’ great-grandmother Haddie comes to visit, Catherine borrows her locket while Haddie is away on a short trip, and the locket goes missing. It is the last keepsake Haddie has of her late husband. Catherine dreads telling her, until an old map leads to an adventure and a possible way to make things right.

  • Paperback, 75 pages
  • Reading age 7 to 12
Amazon
Book 5 · 2026

Laura’s Locker

En Route Books and Media

Susanna Mae sees a classmate framed for cheating. She knows what happened, but speaking up could cost her a friendship. During a visit to her grandparents in Gloucester, Massachusetts, she encounters mysterious woods and old legends. Back at school, the harder challenge remains.

“Michael LaPenna invites young readers into a compelling encounter with conscience, courage, and grace. Laura’s Locker reveals how faith strengthens the heart to confront injustice, embrace truth, and discover the healing power of authentic friendship.”

Dr. Sebastian Mahfood, OP, author of The Narrative Spirituality of Dante’s Divine Comedy
  • Paperback, 191 pages
  • Kindle and Audible
PaperbackKindleAudiblePublisher

Where to begin

The first books are shorter, with longer and more complex stories as the series progresses. Readers can begin with Baby Jesus Is Missing and follow Catherine and Susanna Mae as they grow.
