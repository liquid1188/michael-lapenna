About

Michael LaPenna

Michael LaPenna grew up on the North Shore of Massachusetts and attended Colby College in Waterville, Maine. He later earned a master’s degree in American literature from Salem State University.

For 34 years, Michael taught secondary school English at a public school north of Boston, where he also coached basketball and baseball.

Since retiring, Michael has turned his attention to stories for young readers. His LaRosa Sisters series follows Catherine and Susanna Mae as they solve mysteries and wrestle with questions about honesty, friendship, and faith.

Michael and his wife, Patti, live in upstate South Carolina and spend part of each year in western Maine and southeastern Florida. Away from his writing desk, he enjoys visiting his three granddaughters, splitting wood, reading, and keeping up with Boston sports.
