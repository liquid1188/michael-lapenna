For schools

Creative writing workshops

Along with readings, Michael leads creative writing workshops for students, drawing on his 34 years of teaching English.

He has read at St. Mary’s School in Greenville on several occasions and led creative writing workshops for students there, and he has tabled his books at other venues as well. A workshop can be planned on its own or as part of a school visit.

What to expect

  • A reading from a LaRosa Sisters book suited to the audience
  • Time for readers to ask about the stories and characters
  • Book signing when copies are available

Fees and books

Michael lives in the Greenville, South Carolina, area and spends December through April near Fort Lauderdale, Florida, so he is available for readings and signings in both places. He does not charge a speaking fee. For events farther from home, usually out of state, he asks only that his travel and lodging be covered.

He can bring copies of the books to sell and sign at your event. If you would like him to bring books, please give him a few weeks’ notice. Readers can also order copies online ahead of time and bring them to be signed.
