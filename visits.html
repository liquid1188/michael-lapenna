Bookstores

Book signings and readings at bookstores.

A visit can include

  • A reading from one of the LaRosa Sisters books chosen for the age of the group
  • Questions from students and readers
  • Signed copies for readers who bring or buy a book

Pre-planning. Michael to confirm what a visit includes, how long it runs, group sizes, and whether he offers a talk on writing or on his years teaching English.

Which book for which readers

  • Baby Jesus Is MissingReading age 5 to 12
  • The OfferingReading age 4 to 8
  • Angelina GracePaperback, 164 pages
  • Laura’s LockerPaperback, 191 pages

Reading ages are from the publishers’ listings where they give one.

Planning

What helps Michael plan a visit

  1. DatesA date or a window of dates that would work.
  2. ReadersGrade levels or ages, and about how many.
  3. PlaceWhere it is, and the room or space you have in mind.
  4. BooksWhether readers will buy copies, through you, a bookstore, or Michael.

Pre-planning. Michael to confirm his travel area, any fee or honorarium, and whether he does virtual visits.
