About the series

Mysteries with a conscience.

Every LaRosa Sisters book starts with a puzzle. A thief is stealing baby Jesus figurines from Nativity scenes all over town. A border collie bolts after a bear cub into forbidden woods. A young woman is living alone in a hollow outside town. A classmate is framed for cheating.

Solving each one asks something of Catherine and her younger sister, Susanna Mae. They have to be brave, tell the truth, and decide what is right when it costs them something. The sisters grow up as the series goes on, and the books grow with them.
