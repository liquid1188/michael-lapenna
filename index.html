About the series

The stories behind the mysteries

Someone is stealing baby Jesus figures from Nativity scenes in Reedy River. In the mountains, Atticus the border collie chases a bear cub into forbidden woods. Elsewhere, a young woman living alone needs help, and a schoolgirl is accused of cheating. Catherine and Susanna Mae find themselves drawn into each mystery.

Each adventure asks the sisters to think beyond the clues and consider the people involved. Questions of honesty, loyalty, friendship, and faith become more complicated as Catherine and Susanna Mae grow older.

“Michael LaPenna invites young readers into a compelling encounter with conscience, courage, and grace. Laura’s Locker reveals how faith strengthens the heart to confront injustice, embrace truth, and discover the healing power of authentic friendship.”

Dr. Sebastian Mahfood, OP, author of The Narrative Spirituality of Dante’s Divine Comedy
