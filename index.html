Catholic mysteries for young readers

Two sisters, a small town, and the courage to do what’s right.

Michael LaPenna writes the LaRosa Sisters series, mysteries for young readers in which Catherine and Susanna Mae LaRosa solve puzzles and learn about faith, friendship, and telling the truth.

About the author

Thirty-four years in the classroom

Michael LaPenna grew up on the North Shore of Massachusetts and taught secondary school English for thirty-four years. Now he writes for children.
