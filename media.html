Short bio

Michael LaPenna is the author of the LaRosa Sisters mystery series for young readers. He graduated from Colby College and earned a master’s degree in American literature from Salem State University. After 34 years teaching secondary school English and coaching basketball and baseball north of Boston, he now lives in upstate South Carolina.
